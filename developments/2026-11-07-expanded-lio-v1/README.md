# expanded_lio_v1 (7 November 2026)

Expanded version, in Journal of Number Theory (elsarticle) format, of
*Rational Resonances and Microscopic Natural--Logarithmic Profiles for the
Liouville Function*. Sections 1--6 and the numerical sections are the
original text (built from `tex_resonant_liouville_JNT_FINAL.tex`, the
pre-v2 text); Sections 7--9 are new.

- **Section 7.** Unconditional natural profile with rate `(log X)^{-kappa}`,
  using Theorem 1.1 of the OpenAI preprint *Ordinary two-point correlations
  of multiplicative functions* (24 Sept 2026) for the finitely many fixed
  binary blocks of the residue-class decomposition.
- **Section 8.** The k-point resonance family `C^{(k)}_X`: exact
  decomposition into proportionality classes, profile, criterion for
  unconditional closure, plateaus/majority/left-hand identity at every k,
  level three at (2,3) = ordinary three-point sum on `M = 3 mod 6`,
  necessity theorem, odd levels in logarithmic average.
- **Section 9.** The centering step of the bias method for k >= 3: barriers
  (Props 9.2, 9.3), unconditional control of the k-point centering error by
  local `U^k` norms (Prop 9.6), and a conditional k-point theorem under two
  explicit hypotheses, `LU_k` (quantitative local Gowers uniformity) and
  `G_k` (k-linear divisor-graph estimate). Neither hypothesis is proved.

Files: `expanded_lio_v1.tex` / `.txt` (identical content) / `.pdf`, the three
PNG figures, `expanded_lio_v1.zip` (everything needed to compile), and
`new_sections_source.tex` (source of Sections 7--9). Compile with pdflatex,
three passes (pdfcomment tooltips).
